AOTD: Manifest (unfinished page)

Manifest is a Turkish girl group formed by the talent agency Hypers New Media in 2025. It consists of Esin Bahat, Hilal Yelekçi, Lidya Pınar, Mina Solak, Sueda Uluca, and Zeynep Sude Oktay, and they play a mix of pop, dance, and R&B.

How It Began: The Big5 Türkiye Process

The story of Manifest began with producer Tolga Akış's vision to create a new girl group in Turkiye under the Hypers label. The project, aiming to replicate the global success of Turkish TV series in the music industry, was announced on April 9, 2024, and transformed into a six-month reality show competition called Big5 Türkiye, broadcast on YouTube. From 60 candidates aged 18-24, 12 finalists were selected after preliminary rounds, a camp period, and three grand final episodes. Although the group was initially planned to consist of five members, a surprise development occurred at the grand final on January 30th, with the unanimous decision of the jury members, resulting in six contestants being chosen as winners.

A Dream Turning into a Name: Manifest

Having gone through a challenging and intense preparation process, the members define the core philosophy of the group as 'women's solidarity.' Throughout the competition, they frequently spoke about manifesting this dream and often expressed their awe at the coincidences that unfolded along the way. That is why they unanimously decided to name the group 'Manifest.'
